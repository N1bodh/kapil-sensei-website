✅ Kapil Sensei does not set its own first-party tracking cookies.

However, third-party services embedded in this website (YouTube, Cloudflare, Google Fonts) may set their own cookies or process technical data. This notice explains each.

4.1 What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that websites may store on your device. They help websites remember your preferences, maintain sessions, and count visits.

Some cookies are essential for a website to function correctly. Others are used for analytics, advertising or personalisation and are set by third-party services embedded in a website.

Browser-based storage technologies (such as localStorage) work in a similar way to cookies but remain on your device and are not automatically sent to a server with each request.

4.2 Our Own Cookie Usage

Kapil Sensei does not currently set its own first-party tracking cookies.

If a cookie-based session or preference feature is introduced in the future, this notice will be updated to explain its purpose, duration and the choices available.

4.3 Browser localStorage (Student Account Data)

🗂️ Browser localStorage First-Party · Device Only

Student account information (name, email, goal preference) entered on this website is stored in your browser's localStorage. This data is saved only on your device and is not transmitted to our servers. You can view, edit or delete it through your browser's developer tools or by clearing your browser storage. localStorage data persists until you clear it or uninstall the browser.

🎨 Theme Preference First-Party · Device Only

Your chosen display theme (dark or light mode) may be saved in localStorage so the site remembers your preference on future visits. This data stays on your device only.

4.4 Cloudflare (Website Hosting)

🛡️ Cloudflare Workers & CDN Infrastructure

This website is hosted on Cloudflare Workers. Cloudflare may process technical information (including IP addresses and device data) to deliver and protect the website. Cloudflare may set a security cookie (typically __cf_bm) for bot detection and DDoS protection. This is a functional security measure. For details, see Cloudflare's Privacy Policy.

4.5 YouTube Video Embeds

▶️ YouTube (Google LLC) Third-Party

Our website includes embedded YouTube videos for Japanese-language lessons. When a YouTube video is played (or in some cases, when the page loads), YouTube may set cookies on your device and collect technical data in accordance with Google's terms and privacy policy.

YouTube cookies may be used for:

  • Tracking video plays and user engagement.
  • Serving personalised or contextual advertising (on YouTube's platforms).
  • Maintaining session state while watching videos.

You can manage YouTube / Google cookies through your Google account settings or by using your browser's cookie controls. See Google's Privacy Policy for details.

4.6 Google Fonts

🔤 Google Fonts Third-Party · CDN

This website loads fonts (Inter, Outfit) from Google Fonts CDN to display clean, modern typography. Google may process your IP address and browser information when fonts are loaded. Google states it does not use font requests for advertising purposes. See Google Fonts Privacy FAQ for details.

4.7 Analytics and Tracking

This website does not currently use Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, or other analytics tracking services.

If analytics tools are added in the future, this notice will be updated to describe the provider, purpose, data collected and how to opt out.

4.8 Your Browser Controls

You can manage cookie and storage preferences through your browser settings. Options typically include:

  • View cookies: See which cookies are set for a page.
  • Delete cookies: Remove existing cookies from your device.
  • Block third-party cookies: Prevent advertising and tracking cookies from third-party domains.
  • Clear localStorage: Remove locally stored data from your browser.
  • Private / incognito mode: Browse without saving cookies or local storage across sessions.

Blocking certain cookies may affect the functionality of embedded YouTube videos or third-party services on this website. Our own website features (localStorage-based) will remain unaffected by cookie blocking.

Browser-specific instructions are available from providers such as Google Chrome and Mozilla Firefox.

This Cookie and Technology Notice will be updated if the technologies used on the website change — for example, if analytics services, authentication systems or advertising features are added.

The current version and its update date are shown at the top of this page.
