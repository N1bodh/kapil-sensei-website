Please read carefully: This disclaimer applies to all educational content, SSW guidance, JLPT/JFT-Basic preparation materials, and career information published on this website. Kapil Sensei is an independent educational resource — not an official examination body, government authority, employer, or licensed recruitment agency.

3.1 Educational Purpose

Kapil Sensei provides educational materials and guidance intended to support Japanese-language learning, JLPT and JFT-Basic preparation, and general understanding of Japan's Specified Skilled Worker (SSW or 特定技能) programmes.

Our content may include Japanese vocabulary, grammar, practice questions, interview preparation, sector-specific learning materials and information about Japanese workplace culture.

This website is an independent educational resource.

Unless explicitly stated and verifiably established, Kapil Sensei is not an official representative of the Japanese government, an official JLPT or JFT-Basic examination body, an immigration authority, an employer, or a licensed recruitment agency.

3.2 No Guarantee of Examination Success

Our lessons and practice materials are designed to support learning. They do not guarantee that a student will pass the JLPT, JFT-Basic, an SSW skills examination or any other assessment.

Examination results depend on individual preparation, proficiency, test conditions, assessment criteria and other factors.

Students should consult the relevant official examination provider for current eligibility requirements, examination dates, fees, test arrangements and results.

3.3 No Guarantee of Employment

Kapil Sensei does not guarantee a job offer, employment contract, recruitment placement, salary, working conditions or selection by a Japanese employer.

Any discussion of job opportunities, salaries or demand in particular industries is for general informational and educational purposes. Actual opportunities depend on employers, locations, qualifications, experience, language ability and current market conditions.

Students should independently verify employer details and contractual terms before accepting an offer.

3.4 No Guarantee of a Visa or Immigration Approval

Passing an examination, completing a course, receiving a job offer or meeting a stated qualification does not automatically guarantee a visa, residence status, Certificate of Eligibility (COE), entry into Japan or approval of any immigration application.

Immigration decisions are made by the competent Japanese authorities under the rules applicable at the time.

Requirements may change. Students should consult official Japanese immigration sources or a properly qualified professional for individual immigration advice.

Our educational resources may discuss SSW sectors. Sector-specific tests, language requirements, eligibility rules and procedures may differ for each sector:

Students must verify the current requirements for their intended sector through the relevant official Japanese authorities and examination providers.

Information published on this website may not always reflect the latest official position.

Where a decision has significant financial, educational, employment or immigration consequences, verify the relevant information before acting.

Our materials should be used as a learning aid rather than a substitute for official instructions, professional advice or independent verification.
