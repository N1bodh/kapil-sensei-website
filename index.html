COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about learning Japanese and working in Japan through SSW.

Both JFT-Basic (A2 level) and JLPT N4 are officially recognized by Japan's Immigration Services Agency for the SSW (Specified Skilled Worker) visa. The major difference is that JFT-Basic is computer-based (CBT) and held multiple times throughout the year with quick result announcements, whereas JLPT is a paper-and-pencil test held only twice a year (July and December).

With consistent daily study of 2–3 hours following Kapil Sensei’s video lessons, most motivated students successfully pass within 4 to 6 months from zero.

Kaigo (Caregiving) has consistently high quota demands and excellent long-term career support with potential transitions to Kaigo certified care worker visa. Food Service (Gaisyoku) and Agriculture (Nōgyō) also offer straightforward skills test syllabi.
