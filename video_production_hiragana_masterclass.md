### [01:10 - 08:30] PART 4: THE 5 VOWELS MASTERCLASS

#### 1. Character 1: あ (A)
* **Visual:** Full-screen animated stroke order drawing:
  - Stroke 1: Horizontal line left-to-right (─)
  - Stroke 2: Downward vertical curving slash (│)
  - Stroke 3: Graceful looped spiral circle (↺)
  - Clean card displaying:
    - **Kana:** `あ`
    - **Romaji:** `a`
    - **Sound Anchor:** Open "Ah" like in *father* or Nepali *"आ"*.
* **Sensei Voice-Over:**  
  > *"First is 'あ' (A).  
  > Pronunciation: Keep your throat relaxed and open your mouth vertically, not too wide. Listen and repeat: あ (A)... あ (A).  
  > Stroke Order: Always write with three intentional strokes. One: horizontal bar. Two: straight center drop with a slight curve. Three: start from the left, loop around like an infinity loop, and finish smoothly. Look at the balance: keep the loop airy and rounded."*

#### 2. Character 2: い (I)
* **Visual:** Animated stroke order:
  - Stroke 1: Left curved line ending in an upward flick (*Hane* ⤴)
  - Stroke 2: Shorter parallel curved line on the right.
  - Card displaying:
    - **Kana:** `い`
    - **Romaji:** `i`
    - **Sound Anchor:** Crisp "Ee" like in *feet* or Nepali *"इ"*.
* **Sensei Voice-Over:**  
  > *"Next is 'い' (I).  
  > Pronunciation: Pull the corners of your mouth slightly outward into a gentle smile. It is a short, sharp, clean sound: い (I)... い (I).  
  > Stroke Order: Two strokes. The left stroke curves down and flicks upward slightly at the bottom. The right stroke is shorter and gently bows inwards. Remember: the left stroke is always slightly longer than the right stroke."*

#### 3. Character 3: う (U) — THE GOLDEN TIP
* **Visual:** Highlighted banner: `⚠️ SENSEI'S CRUCIAL TIP`.  
  Crossed-out icon showing puckered lips (❌ English "Oooo").  
  Correct icon showing unrounded neutral lips (✓ Japanese [ɯ]).
  - Stroke 1: Slanted top accent dash (` `)
  - Stroke 2: Smooth crescent ear curve (`⊃`)
* **Sensei Voice-Over:**  
  > *"Now pay close attention to 'う' (U). This is where foreign learners make the most frequent mistake.  
  > In English, when you say 'Food' or 'Blue', your lips round into a tight circle: 'Oooo'. But in Japanese, your lips do NOT round! Keep your lips relaxed and flat, and release the sound from the middle of your mouth: う (U)... う (U).  
  > Stroke Order: Stroke one is a short diagonal accent at the top. Stroke two is a sweeping, gentle crescent curve directly beneath it. Beautiful and simple."*

#### 4. Character 4: え (E)
* **Visual:** Animated stroke order:
  - Stroke 1: Small diagonal roof tick
  - Stroke 2: Continuous zigzag line (Down-left, sharp diagonal up-right, drop down, into a smooth resting wave `~`)
  - Card displaying:
    - **Kana:** `え`
    - **Romaji:** `e`
    - **Sound Anchor:** Short, crisp "Eh" like in *pen* or Nepali *"ए"*.
* **Sensei Voice-Over:**  
  > *"Fourth is 'え' (E).  
  > Pronunciation: Like the 'e' in 'pen' or 'bed'. It is never drawn out into a diphthong like 'ay'. It is crisp: え (E)... え (E).  
  > Stroke Order: Start with the top dash. Then, in one continuous stroke, slide diagonal left, surge upward to the right, drop down, and glide flat at the base like a calm ocean wave. Do not lift your pen during stroke two!"*

#### 5. Character 5: お (O)
* **Visual:** Animated stroke order:
  - Stroke 1: Top horizontal line
  - Stroke 2: Vertical drop, loop back up and wrap around to the right
  - Stroke 3: Detached diagonal accent dot on the top right
  - Side-by-side comparison with `あ` highlighting differences so viewers never confuse them.
* **Sensei Voice-Over:**  
  > *"And the fifth vowel is 'お' (O).  
  > Pronunciation: Form a medium circle with your lips, but keep the sound short and direct: お (O)... お (O).  
  > Beginner Trap: Notice how 'お' and 'あ' look similar. Here is the distinction: 'あ' has a separate vertical drop that cuts through the center. 'お' has an integrated center loop and finishes with a separate accent dot on the top right. Master this difference now, and you will never confuse them again."*
